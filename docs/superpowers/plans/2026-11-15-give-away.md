# Give Away Free Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** an operator records recyclables or compost leaving free, with no price, from the kiosk or the website. It's reported apart from sales.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-give-away-design.md`

## Global Constraints
- **The new outgoing type `giveaway`:** material + area required; a price is refused with 422 "A give-away has no price."
- **Compost:** the price stays optional.
- **Rules:** a giveaway takes stock at once (`_available` / `_move_out`) and waits for review like a sale. Its income is 0 (`total` stays null).
- **Labels:** "Recyclables given away" (web, reports). Kiosk strings in EN/TL.
- **Process:** never commit `config.h`. Don't `pio run` during the user's upload. SapulMRF commits only on "commit".

## Review Focus
1. **A giveaway with a price:** 422. *Test.*
2. **A giveaway of more than the area holds:** 409. *Test.*
3. **Reports:** the outgoing report labels it; the income report doesn't include it. *Test.*
4. **Kiosk:** switching Sell → Give free → Sell keeps the kg and buyer, and the price comes back. *Live by eye.*
5. **Compost sold on the kiosk:** the price is required once Sell is chosen. *Live by eye.*

### Task 1: API
- **Migration** (down_revision `c5e7a9b1d302`): replace `ck_outgoing_type` with `type IN ('sale','compost','disposal','giveaway')`.
- **`approvals/models.py`:** the same CHECK.
- **`approvals/schemas.py`:**
  - `OutgoingIn.type` adds "giveaway"
  - the validator: a giveaway needs material + area, and has no price
- **`operations/schemas.py` + `service.py`:** `OutgoingView.given_kg_month` (giveaway kg, less reversals).
- **`reports/builders.py`:** the `label` map adds giveaway: "Recyclables given away".
- **Tests (`api/tests/test_giveaway.py`):**
  - leaves stock at once and waits for review
  - a price is refused
  - more than held is refused
  - the outgoing report label, and the income unchanged
  - the outgoing view `given_kg_month`

### Task 2: Web
- **`api/approvals.ts`:** `Outgoing['type']` + 'giveaway'; `OUTGOING_TYPE.giveaway = 'Recyclables given away'`.
- **The operator `NewOutgoingSheet`:**
  - a giveaway radio
  - material + area like a sale, no price field
  - the compost price hint: "Leave empty if given free"
- **The admin Outgoing page:** a "Given away" KPI.
- **e2e:** `operator-01` records a 1 kg giveaway, and it shows "Recorded".
- **Run:** tsc + Playwright.

### Task 3: Bridge
- `OUTGOING_KINDS` + "giveaway".
- The `outgoing` body:
  - giveaway → material + area, no price
  - compost → price when given
- `stock` with kind giveaway lists material rows (as a sale does).
- Extend `check_outgoing.py`.

### Task 4: Kiosk
- **Tab 0** renamed "Recyclables" (`S_K_SALE` text).
- **A `sellFree` flag and a two-button switch** at the top of the form's left column (y 96, 2 × 190 × 52). The step rows move down to y 156, 220 and 284 (h 58). The total goes to y 352.
- **Defaults:** recyclables Sell, compost Give free; residual has no switch.
- **The `outgoing` kind sent:**
  - recyclables + free → "giveaway"
  - compost → "compost" (+ price when Sell)
- **Save text:** free gives "Save · %s kg · free". The receipt shows "Given free" when there's no total and it isn't a disposal.
- **Run:** build; the user uploads; the live check is a 1 kg giveaway → receipt → reverse.
